# Squishy Block — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 November 2026
**Developer:** Veldenix
**Contact:** [vgame@veldenix.com](mailto:vgame@veldenix.com)

This policy explains how the mobile game **Squishy Block** ("the game") handles information on **Android (Google Play)** and **iOS (Apple App Store)**. It applies to every version of the game on these platforms.

## Summary

- The game has **no accounts, no sign-in and no chat**. We (Veldenix) do not run servers for the game and do not collect, receive or sell your personal information.
- Your progress and settings are **stored only on your device**.
- The game shows **ads from Google AdMob**. Google may collect device and usage information to show, measure and protect ads, as described below and in Google's own privacy policy.
- Ads are only requested after the ad privacy choices required in your region have been collected, and you can change those choices at any time in **Settings › Privacy Options** (shown where the rules require it).

## 1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following **locally on your device** so you can continue where you left off:

- best score, the current run (board, pieces and score) and tutorial progress;
- settings such as sound, volume and haptics.

This information never leaves your device through us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game (or clear the app's data). The game does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads delivered by **Google AdMob** (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) with Google's **User Messaging Platform** for consent:

- **Rewarded ads** play only when you choose them (for example "Fresh Set — Watch ad" or "Keep Playing"). You can always continue without watching.
- **Full-screen ads** may appear occasionally between runs. They never appear during the tutorial or while you are playing a run.
- There are no banner ads.

To deliver ads, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

- your device's **advertising identifier** (Android Advertising ID or, on iOS, the IDFA — only if you allow it, see section 3);
- **IP address**, which may be used to estimate your general (not precise) location;
- **device and app information** such as device model, operating system version, language, app version and network type;
- **ad interaction data** such as which ads were shown or tapped, and diagnostic information used for performance and fraud prevention.

Depending on your choices and your region, Google may use this information to show **personalized** or **non-personalized** ads. Google acts under its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- Google advertising and ad personalization controls: <https://adssettings.google.com>

### Your ad privacy choices

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** before ads are requested, the game shows Google's consent message so you can accept, reject or manage how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings › Privacy Options**.
- **US states with privacy laws:** where required, the game shows Google's privacy message and lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising through **Settings › Privacy Options**.
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads, depending on the device).
- **iOS:** see section 3.

## 3. iOS: App Tracking Transparency

On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for your permission through Apple's **App Tracking Transparency** prompt before the advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose **Ask App Not to Track**, the IDFA is not shared and ads remain non-tracking. You can change this at any time in **iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking**.

## 4. App store and platform information

Google Play and the Apple App Store may provide us with **aggregated, non-identifying** statistics (such as install counts, crash reports and performance data) according to their own privacy policies and the settings on your device. We use this only to fix problems and improve the game.

## 5. Permissions

On Android the game uses: **Internet** and **network state** (to load ads), **Advertising ID** (used by Google AdMob as described above) and **vibration** (for gentle haptics, which you can turn off in Settings).

## 6. Children

Squishy Block is a general-audience puzzle game and is **not directed at children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

## 7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with anyone, because we do not collect it. Information collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google as described in section 2.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests are handled by:

- uninstalling the game or clearing its data (deletes everything stored on your device);
- **Settings › Privacy Options** in the game (ad consent and opt-out choices);
- your device's advertising settings and Google's controls linked above.

For any other question or request, email **vgame@veldenix.com**. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 9. Security and retention

Game data stays on your device and is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Information processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes (for example when adding new platforms or features). The latest version is always available at <https://vgame-ux.github.io/SquishyBlock-mobile/privacy-policy.html>, with its effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.
